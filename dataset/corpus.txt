The stars are dancing in the night,
The moon is painting dreams of light.
A little hope begins to grow,
Like seeds beneath the winter snow.

The winds are carrying stories untold,
Of distant dreams and hearts of gold.
Through every challenge, we arise,
And find our strength beyond the skies.

A quiet thought, a gentle breeze,
A thousand whispers through the trees.
The world is full of things to see,
And endless possibilities.

When morning spills across the sky,
The sleepy clouds go drifting by.
A thousand paths begin anew,
Beneath a sky of endless blue.

Keep a little wonder in your heart,
Let imagination be your art.
Every question, every try,
Can teach your wings to learn to fly.

The river moves beyond the stone,
It finds a way to make its own.
And even when the road is long,
A patient heart can still be strong.
